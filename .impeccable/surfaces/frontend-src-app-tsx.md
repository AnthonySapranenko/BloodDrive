# Public drive directory

Scope: the public home page of BloodDrive, listing upcoming drives with state/city/ZIP filters. Mode: Operate.

Audience and task: State College students on phones finding a drive they can attend and reaching its official sign-up link. Date and venue share top billing. Must label demo and cancelled drives unmistakably, show times in each drive's own timezone, and mark registration as an external link.

Avoid: hospital/clinical look, gore, too much red.

Unresolved: no pagination; no distance search.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The directory reads like a festival lineup: the soonest drive headlines at poster scale, later drives step down in tiers (this week, next week, later). Refuses the stack of identical white cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ultramarine #2F3DD9 top field, periwinkle-white #F3F4FF ground, ink #0D1033, marigold #FFB627 reserved for the Register action only. One family, Archivo, its width axis doing the tiering (condensed caps for headliner venues, wide caps for labels). Hairline ink rules split tiers. Square-ish 4px corners. No grey text below AA.

STORY: The student sees the next drive instantly, knows when and where, and taps Register to the official page; scrolls to see what follows; filters to their town.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ultramarine header with BLOODDRIVE wordmark, one-line welcome, and state/city/ZIP filters in a row. Below, the headliner tier: label "Next up", venue in condensed caps at ~64px, date and local time, town and organizer, marigold Register button plus DEMO tag. Next tier begins above the fold at ~34px.

FORM: Lineup (festival lineup poster), position 3 of my re-rolled list; seed key 291cdc36, re-roll 1. Signature move: tier sizing by date proximity. Raises: hairline tier rules and one family (design annual plates); cancelled as a struck bar plus text tag (centre rail); marigold for the single action only (orienteering); full contrast (datamatics). Motion: none beyond instant state changes; respects reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
